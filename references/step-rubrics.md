# Point rubrics by CFS step

Only CFS 1 has a published point rubric. The Step 2–4 and memo rubrics are **inferred**: each line comes from a syllabus requirement or a recurring feedback pattern, in the same shape as CFS 1 (content lines at 5 points, a strategic-uncertainty line, live presentation). Say "inferred rubric" when using them, and replace a section as soon as a real one is published.

Scoring guide, based on how CFS 1 was graded: full points = specific numbers tied to a decision; about 4 or "Partial" = the right content but the link to the strategy, trade-off or adjustment is missing; 3 or less = generic, unverified or missing.
